I am a reporter, researcher and translator from the Philly suburbs, now living in Mexico City. I hold a master's in Migration Studies from La Universidad Iberoamericana.

My work has appeared in The Nation, Border Chronicle, PRISM Reports, OregonLive, WHYY/Billy Penn, Teen Vogue, AL DÍA News, and the Malheur Enterprise.

Recently, I took a break from reporting and worked as a doula apprentice for a year at Anidar. I hope all of my work reflects what I learned there about accompaniment and care.
